1. Parties
This agreement is made between Alpha Corp ("Disclosing Party") and Beta LLC ("Receiving Party").

2. Confidentiality
The Receiving Party shall keep confidential all information marked as confidential. The Receiving Party must return Confidential Information within 7 days of request.

3. Payment
Alpha Corp shall pay Beta LLC $10,000 within thirty (30) days of invoice receipt.

4. Restriction
Receiving Party shall not disclose Confidential Information to third parties.

5. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days written notice.
